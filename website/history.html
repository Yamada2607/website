Wawa Dam

Location: Barangay San Rafael, Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal

Background: Wawa Dam is a historic dam located along the Marikina River near the scenic Wawa Gorge. It was constructed during the American colonial period and was once an important source of water for Manila.

Today, the area is known for its natural rock formations, surrounding mountains, and outdoor recreation. It attracts visitors who enjoy sightseeing, photography, and hiking.

Wawa Dam is an important landmark that connects local history with the natural beauty of Rodriguez, Rizal.
